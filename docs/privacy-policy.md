# Privacy Policy

**Unit Converter for iPhone and iPad** · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Unit Converter app for iPhone and iPad (the "App")
handles your information. Your privacy is important to us, and we have built the App to work
entirely on your device.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal information.** The App
performs every conversion locally on your device. It does not require an account, and it does
not connect to the internet.

- No personal data is collected.
- No usage analytics or tracking is performed.
- No advertising identifiers are used, and the App contains no ads.
- No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

## Information Stored on Your Device

To make the App more convenient, it saves a few preferences in its own private storage on your
device:

- the last value you typed in each category, so the App can pick up where you left off
- categories and conversions you pin to the home screen, and the order of your categories
- your chosen number of decimal places, and the work week you set for the Pay category
- cities you add to the Clock

This information never leaves your device through the App, and we have no access to it. Like
other app data, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup),
which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the App removes this data from your
device.

## Permissions

The App does not ask for any permissions. It has no access to your location, contacts, photos,
camera, microphone, calendars or files, and it does not use the internet.

The Clock uses the time-zone setting already on your device to show your local time. It does
**not** request or use your location.

## Copying and Sharing

The App only puts text on your clipboard when you choose to copy a value or a whole conversion,
and only opens the share sheet when you tap Share. Where shared content goes is decided by you
and the app or service you pick.

## Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from within the App, the text you type is passed to your own
email app so you can send it to us. The App itself does not store or transmit your message —
sending the email is handled entirely by the email app you use, through your email provider. If
you contact us this way, we will see the email address and message you send, and use them only
to respond to your feedback.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page
with an updated revision date.
